## Interpretation

This is a regression gate, not proof that all repositories are unaffected. It covers three real upstream families across two projects and the main short-trajectory shapes: direct patch, read then patch, and recovery from a rejected localized replacement. Together with V4.1's positive trigger gate, it shows both sides of the policy contract: the grace activates on the previously blocked unread search hit, and remains inactive when existing evidence is already sufficient.
